# warrant.md : when to write a letter, and how the user's letters read

## Warrant test
A letter costs the user's review time and a recruiter's attention, so write one when it changes the
outcome:
- the role is senior or leadership (Head of, Director, Lead, Principal, founder-adjacent
  operator, C-level);
- the application asks for one (a cover-letter field, "tell us why", "a covering letter is
  required");
- the fit needs a bridge the CV cannot make alone: a career change, a remote or
  relocation point, or a specific mission match worth stating.
Skip it for quick or easy-apply flows with no letter field, junior or transactional roles the CV
already covers, and roles where the letter would only repeat the CV.

## Shape
Three or four short paragraphs, 250 to 380 words of body text, one page.
1. Opening: the role, and the single most relevant proof point for this employer. Not always
   the same headline figure: pick what this advert cares about most.
2. Fit: two or three concrete, quantified points from the profile that answer the advert's
   priorities, in the advert's own terms where they are honestly the candidate's.
3. Why this employer: a real, specific reason tied to the role or mission. Leave the paragraph
   out rather than write a hollow one.
4. Close: short and direct. No "References available on request".
Salutation: the named person if the advert gives one; otherwise a plain "Dear Hiring Manager" or
"Dear <Company> team".

## The candidate's voice
- Describe the voice once, in the master profile or in config/application_rules.md (for example:
  "plain, direct, evidence first: what shipped, what grew, then the adjective if any"), and keep
  every letter in it.
- Be honest about the kind of practitioner the profile describes. If the profile says the
  candidate builds with AI assistance and is not a software engineer, say so plainly where it
  matters and never imply engineering depth.
- British English, no em dashes, no dashes as punctuation.
- Every claim traces to profile/master_profile.md. Leave gaps as gaps; never invent a metric,
  a duty or a network.
- Titles follow the employer_rules in config/application_rules.md.

## De-slop
Run avoid-ai-writing over the draft to remove AI tells (stock trait claims, inflated
significance, uniform rhythm, weak openers) while keeping the candidate's voice. Letters and emails only:
never on a CV, where a generic pass strips the advert keywords the ATS needs.
